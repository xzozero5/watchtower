# Reset your password

If you cannot sign in because you forgot your password, you can set a new one yourself. This takes about two minutes.

## Steps

1. Open the sign-in page and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address of your account.
3. Open the message we send you and choose **Set a new password**.
4. Pick a new password and sign in with it.

## Good to know

- The link works once and expires after 15 minutes. If it has expired, ask for a new one.
- After five wrong attempts an account is locked for one hour. Resetting the password does not shorten the lock.
- If no message arrives within a few minutes, check your spam folder and make sure you used the address the account was created with.

## Still stuck?

Contact support with the email address of the account. Never send your password to us: we will never ask for it.
