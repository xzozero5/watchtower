# Invoices and billing questions

## When invoices are issued

Invoices are issued on the first day of each month for the previous month and sent to the billing contact of your organization. You can also download them from the billing page of your account.

## Change the billing contact

Ask an administrator of your organization to update the billing contact on the billing page. The next invoice goes to the new address.

## A charge you do not recognise

Send us the invoice number and the line you are asking about. We will check the usage behind it and reply with a breakdown. Please do not send card numbers or full bank details: we never need them to look into a charge.
